upatches
========

These pages are for upatch authors: engineers who turn a kernel fix or
an observation into a signed upatch and ship it as a package. Start with
:doc:`/upatches/concepts`, then follow
:doc:`/upatches/kernel-fix-tutorial` from a real kernel fix to a
published package. :doc:`/upatches/attach-kinds` and
:doc:`/upatches/beefile` are the references to keep at hand, and
:doc:`/upatches/debugging` explains each way a load can fail.
